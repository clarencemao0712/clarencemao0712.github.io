Rapid Prototyping of a puzzle platformer with cloning and teleport mechanic to solve puzzles.

Controls:

Left/Right Movement: A and D

Jump: W

Reset Level: R

Waypoint/Clone and Teleport: Space Bar

Open Menu: ESC

Description:

Prototype single player puzzle game that aims to solve 8 different levels with progressing difficulties. Explore the ability to trigger contraptions with a clone and complete the puzzle. Game designed and developed by myself.

Used Unity and C# to develop the game alongside free unity assets from Simple 2D Platformer Assets Pack by Goldmetal
