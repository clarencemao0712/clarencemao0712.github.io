Description:

A 3D platformer where players use a rope mechanic to catch wandering spirits. This team project showcases custom sound design and robust gameplay programming to create an engaging spirit-catching experience.

Controls:

Movement: WASD

Jump: Space

Aim/Throw Rope: Hold Left Click To Aim, Release to throw

Open Menu: ESC

Key Contributions:

Level designs, sound effects, evil spirit, rope components, and maintenance

Used Blender, Unity, and C# to develop the game alongside free assets from opengameart.org, pixabay.com, sketchfab.com, and Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 4.0 International by David den Ouden
